IdeaHub AI - Product Demonstrator
Team: Team0022
Product: IdeaHub AI
Course: MBA ZG583 - Management of AI Products

1. Purpose
This demonstrator illustrates the proposed IdeaHub AI product experience.
IdeaHub AI helps enterprise transformation teams convert:
• business-submitted transformation ideas; and
• Process Mining or process-analysis findings
into structured, explainable and human-governed transformation opportunity assessments.
The demonstrator focuses on the core product journey:
Opportunity Intake → AI Interpretation → Clarification → Assessment → Intervention Recommendation → Human Review

2. How to Run
No installation or backend service is required.
Option 1 - Run Locally
1. Download index.html.
2. Open the file using a modern web browser such as Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
3. Select a scenario from the drop-down menu.
4. Navigate through the six stages using the application controls.
Option 2 - GitHub Pages
The HTML file can also be hosted using GitHub Pages to provide browser-based access without downloading the file.

3. Demonstration Scenarios
The product demonstrator contains four synthetic scenarios.
All scenarios and associated figures are illustrative and are not presented as real customer data.

Scenario A - Vendor Invoice Approval
Entry Path: Business Idea
The opportunity contains:
• structured invoice approval activities; and
• unstructured supplier correspondence and exceptions.
IdeaHub AI recommends:
Workflow Automation + Generative AI Assistance

This scenario demonstrates that different parts of the same process may require different intervention types.
It also demonstrates that Agentic AI should not be recommended when the evidence does not justify autonomous multi-step execution.

Scenario B - Employee Leave Approval
Entry Path: Business Idea
The stakeholder requests AI agents for employee leave approval.
However, the process has:
• explicit policies;
• structured information;
• predictable rules; and
• limited need for interpretation.
IdeaHub AI recommends:
Deterministic Business Rules + Workflow Automation

This scenario demonstrates a core IdeaHub AI product principle:
Artificial Intelligence should not be recommended when a simpler intervention adequately solves the business problem.

Scenario C - Customer Complaint Resolution
Entry Path: Business Idea
The workflow requires:
• gathering customer information across multiple systems;
• interpreting context;
• selecting a resolution;
• updating systems; and
• potentially taking financial actions.
IdeaHub AI recommends:
Bounded Agentic AI with Human Approval for Financial Actions

This scenario demonstrates where Agentic AI may create value while still requiring defined autonomy boundaries and Human-in-the-Loop governance.

Scenario D - Repeated Approval Loop
Entry Path: Process Mining Finding
A synthetic Process Mining observation identifies repeated purchase-order approval activity and additional waiting time.
IdeaHub AI evaluates the process evidence and recommends:
Process Redesign + Mandatory-Field Validation Rules

This scenario demonstrates the relationship between Process Mining and IdeaHub AI.
Process Mining identifies the process signal.
IdeaHub AI helps determine:
• whether the issue is worth addressing;
• what the likely problem is;
• which intervention is appropriate; and
• whether Artificial Intelligence is actually necessary.

4. Demonstrated AI Capabilities
The demonstrator represents the following proposed Artificial Intelligence capabilities.
Information Interpretation
IdeaHub AI converts an unstructured opportunity description into structured opportunity information.
Information-Gap Identification
The product identifies material information that is unavailable.
Clarification Generation
IdeaHub AI produces context-specific questions rather than relying only on a fixed assessment form.
Opportunity Classification
The product identifies the primary transformation need.
Intervention Recommendation
IdeaHub AI compares different possible approaches rather than automatically recommending Artificial Intelligence.
Explanation Generation
The product explains:
• evidence;
• assumptions;
• information gaps;
• recommendation rationale; and
• material risk.

5. Human-in-the-Loop Design
The demonstrator intentionally preserves human control.
The user can:
• review AI interpretation;
• edit interpreted information;
• answer clarification questions;
• record information as unknown;
• review the assessment;
• inspect recommendation rationale;
• accept the recommendation;
• modify the recommendation;
• request more information; or
• reject the recommendation.
Where a reviewer modifies or rejects the recommendation, a rationale is required.
The final decision remains human governed.

7. What Is Simulated
The current demonstrator focuses on the product workflow and user experience.
The following behaviours are simulated using predefined scenario responses:
• AI interpretation;
• clarification generation;
• value and feasibility assessment;
• risk assessment;
• evidence-confidence assessment;
• intervention recommendation; and
• explanation generation.
The current demonstrator does not claim production Artificial Intelligence performance.

8. What Is Interactive
The user can:
• select different scenarios;
• move through the complete product journey;
• edit opportunity information;
• edit AI-interpreted information;
• select and deselect identified terms;
• provide clarification responses;
• record information as unknown;
• inspect assessment dimensions;
• expand alternative intervention reasoning;
• review recommendation rationale;
• accept, modify, request more information or reject the recommendation; and
• generate a final human decision record.

9. Process Mining Boundary
IdeaHub AI is not designed to replace a Process Mining platform.
The product can consume Process Mining findings as opportunity-discovery evidence.
The product-management boundary is:
Process Mining identifies what is happening in the process.

IdeaHub AI helps determine what should be done about it.

This allows Process Mining capability to complement rather than duplicate IdeaHub AI.

10. Demonstrator Limitations
The current demonstrator does not include:
• live Large Language Model integration;
• production enterprise data;
• a full Process Mining engine;
• enterprise authentication;
• production database integration;
• automatic funding decisions;
• production financial-benefit calculations; or
• production-grade security and governance controls.
These limitations are intentional because the current objective is to demonstrate and evaluate the IdeaHub AI product concept and decision workflow.

11. Product Principle
The demonstrator is designed around one core principle:
Use Artificial Intelligence to improve transformation decisions, not to maximise the use of Artificial Intelligence.

The strongest recommendation may therefore be an AI solution, conventional automation, process redesign or no intervention depending on the problem and available evidence.
