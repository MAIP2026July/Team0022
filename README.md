Product Overview
IdeaHub AI is an AI-assisted transformation discovery, qualification and prioritisation copilot for enterprise transformation teams.
It helps convert:
1. business-submitted transformation ideas; and
2. Process Mining or process-analysis findings
into structured, explainable and human-governed transformation opportunity assessments.
IdeaHub AI supports assessment across:
• business value;
• feasibility;
• risk;
• evidence strength;
• data readiness; and
• intervention suitability.
Depending on the business problem and available evidence, IdeaHub AI can recommend:
• no intervention;
• further assessment;
• process redesign;
• policy or procedural change;
• deterministic business rules;
• workflow automation;
• Robotic Process Automation;
• Predictive Machine Learning;
• Generative Artificial Intelligence;
• Agentic Artificial Intelligence; or
• a hybrid approach.
The final transformation and investment decision remains with an authorised human reviewer.

Product Principle
Use Artificial Intelligence to improve transformation decisions, not to maximise the use of Artificial Intelligence.

IdeaHub AI is designed to recommend the least complex intervention that adequately addresses the business problem.

Target Users
Primary User
Transformation Analyst / Automation or AI Centre of Excellence Analyst
The primary user reviews incoming opportunities, clarifies missing information, evaluates the opportunity and prepares the initial transformation recommendation.
Decision User
Transformation Lead / Centre of Excellence Lead
The decision user reviews the assessment and decides whether the opportunity should progress, be modified, require more information or be rejected.
Supporting Stakeholders
Supporting stakeholders may include:
• Business idea submitters;
• Process Owners;
• Process Mining Analysts;
• Business Analysts;
• Solution Architects;
• AI Specialists;
• Risk and Compliance Reviewers; and
• Governance / Investment Authorities.

Two Opportunity Entry Paths
1. Business-Led Entry
A stakeholder submits a transformation, automation or AI idea using normal business language.
IdeaHub AI interprets the submission, identifies missing information and helps structure the opportunity for assessment.
2. Process-Led Entry
A Process Mining or process-analysis finding identifies an operational signal such as:
• a bottleneck;
• rework loop;
• long waiting time;
• recurring exception;
• manual hand-off; or
• process deviation.
IdeaHub AI helps convert the finding into a candidate business opportunity before qualification.
Process Mining identifies what is happening in the process. IdeaHub AI helps determine what should be done about it.

Core Product Journey
The initial IdeaHub AI journey consists of:
1. Opportunity Intake
2. AI Interpretation
3. Missing-Information Identification
4. Contextual Clarification
5. Value, Feasibility, Risk and Evidence Assessment
6. Intervention Recommendation
7. Human Review and Decision
Artificial Intelligence supports interpretation, assessment and recommendation.
Human reviewers retain accountability for the final decision.
