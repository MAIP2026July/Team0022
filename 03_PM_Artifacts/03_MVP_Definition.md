IdeaHub AI - Minimum Viable Product Definition
Product: IdeaHub AI
Team: Team0022
Release: R1 - Minimum Viable Product

1. MVP Objective
The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) for IdeaHub AI will validate the core product thesis:
Can AI help enterprise transformation teams convert an unstructured transformation idea into a more structured, explainable and consistently assessed opportunity while keeping the final decision with a human reviewer?

The MVP focuses on qualification of an individual transformation opportunity.
Process Mining will be supported as an additional source of opportunity information, while deeper Process Mining-led discovery and portfolio-level prioritisation can evolve in subsequent releases.

2. User
The primary user of the MVP is a:
Transformation Analyst / Automation or AI Centre of Excellence Analyst
The user receives an opportunity from a business stakeholder or through process analysis and needs to determine whether it deserves further investment.
The Transformation or Centre of Excellence Lead acts as the decision user who reviews the recommendation.

3. MVP Entry Paths
The MVP will support two input paths.
Path 1 - Business Idea
The user submits an existing transformation idea using business language.
Example:
Our invoice approval process has multiple manual checks and suppliers frequently contact us because payments are delayed.

Path 2 - Process Observation
The user provides an existing Process Mining or process-analysis finding.
Example:
Process analysis shows repeated approval loops within the Purchase-to-Pay process, resulting in longer waiting time and additional manual handling.

The MVP will not perform full Process Mining itself.
It will use the process finding as evidence that needs to be translated into and assessed as a transformation opportunity.

4. Minimum Inputs
The MVP will capture the following information where available:
• opportunity title;
• business process;
• problem description;
• affected stakeholder;
• current pain point;
• frequency or volume;
• expected business outcome;
• available supporting evidence; and
• optional Process Mining observation.
Not all information is expected to be available during initial submission.
The ability to identify and handle missing information is therefore part of the MVP.

5. Core MVP Flow
The MVP product journey is:
Step 1 - Submit Opportunity
The user submits a transformation idea or Process Mining observation.
Step 2 - Interpret
IdeaHub AI interprets the submission and structures the available information.
Step 3 - Identify Gaps
IdeaHub AI identifies material information that is missing or unclear.
Step 4 - Clarify
The product generates relevant clarification questions.
The user can provide additional information or continue with the information gap recorded explicitly.
Step 5 - Assess
IdeaHub AI produces an indicative assessment across:
• business value;
• feasibility;
• risk; and
• evidence strength.
Step 6 - Recommend Intervention
IdeaHub AI recommends an appropriate intervention.
Possible recommendations include:
• no intervention;
• further assessment;
• process redesign;
• policy or procedural change;
• deterministic business rules;
• workflow automation;
• Robotic Process Automation;
• Predictive Machine Learning;
• Generative Artificial Intelligence;
• Agentic Artificial Intelligence; or
• a hybrid approach.
Step 7 - Explain
IdeaHub AI explains:
• why the intervention was recommended;
• which information supports the recommendation;
• which assumptions remain;
• what information is missing; and
• which risks require further consideration.
Step 8 - Human Review
The reviewer can:
• accept;
• modify;
• request more information; or
• reject the assessment.
A modified or rejected recommendation should include the reviewer's rationale.
Step 9 - Opportunity Outcome
The reviewed opportunity receives an outcome such as:
• Reject;
• Refine;
• Explore;
• Prototype;
• Pilot; or
• Candidate for Prioritisation.

6. AI Role in the MVP
Artificial Intelligence will be used where interpretation of unstructured information creates value.
AI responsibilities include:
• interpreting opportunity descriptions;
• extracting relevant business information;
• identifying information gaps;
• generating clarification questions;
• classifying the transformation opportunity;
• recommending potential intervention approaches; and
• producing an explainable assessment narrative.
Artificial Intelligence will not independently approve transformation investments.

7. Deterministic Logic
Not every product decision should be controlled by Artificial Intelligence.
Deterministic logic will be preferred for:
• defined assessment criteria;
• score calculations;
• mandatory thresholds;
• governance conditions;
• required approvals; and
• final workflow states.
This combination allows IdeaHub AI to use Artificial Intelligence for interpretation while maintaining consistent controls around decision-making.

8. Human Role in the MVP
Human judgement remains necessary for:
• validating business context;
• confirming important evidence;
• correcting incorrect interpretations;
• changing assumptions;
• overriding assessments;
• reviewing risks;
• accepting or rejecting the recommendation; and
• deciding whether an opportunity should progress.
The MVP therefore uses Artificial Intelligence as a decision-support capability rather than an autonomous decision-maker.

9. Core MVP Outputs
For each opportunity, the MVP should produce:
Opportunity Summary
A structured explanation of the problem, stakeholder and intended outcome.
Missing Information
Important information that is unavailable or requires clarification.
Value Assessment
An indicative assessment of potential business value.
Feasibility Assessment
An indicative assessment of the practicality of the intervention.
Risk Assessment
Material risks and issues requiring further review.
Evidence Status
An indication of whether the available evidence is strong, moderate or limited.
Intervention Recommendation
The recommended transformation approach.
Recommendation Rationale
An explanation of why the intervention was recommended.
Human Decision
The final reviewer decision and, where applicable, the reason for overriding the recommendation.

10. MVP Scope Boundary
The MVP is intentionally focused on the opportunity qualification decision.
It will not initially require:
• enterprise-wide portfolio optimisation;
• detailed project estimation;
• autonomous funding approval;
• autonomous implementation of recommended solutions;
• proprietary foundation-model development;
• complete Process Mining capability; or
• enterprise resource allocation.
These capabilities may be considered in later product releases after the core opportunity qualification journey has demonstrated value.

11. What the MVP Must Demonstrate
The MVP should provide sufficient evidence to assess whether:
1. unstructured transformation ideas can be converted into useful structured assessments;
2. missing information can be identified before recommendations are made;
3. Process Mining observations can be converted into meaningful candidate opportunities;
4. value, feasibility and risk can be presented in a useful and understandable manner;
5. the product can recommend an appropriate intervention without defaulting to Artificial Intelligence;
6. reviewers can understand the reasoning behind the recommendation; and
7. Human-in-the-Loop review provides sufficient control over the final decision.
The purpose of the MVP is therefore to validate the core product decision loop before expanding IdeaHub AI into broader discovery, prioritisation and portfolio-management capabilities.
