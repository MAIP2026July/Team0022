IdeaHub AI - Product Roadmap
Product: IdeaHub AI
Team: Team0022
Roadmap Type: Outcome-Based Product Roadmap

1. Roadmap Purpose
This roadmap describes how IdeaHub AI can evolve from an individual opportunity-qualification product into a broader transformation discovery and portfolio decision-support capability.
The roadmap is outcome-based rather than date-based.
Progression between releases will depend on whether the previous release produces sufficient evidence of:
• user value;
• recommendation quality;
• reviewer trust;
• operational feasibility;
• manageable risk; and
• continued business relevance.
The roadmap does not represent a commitment to build every capability. Each release will require a separate continue, revise or stop decision.

Release 1 - Opportunity Qualification MVP
Release Objective
Validate whether IdeaHub AI can convert an unstructured transformation idea or Process Mining observation into a structured, explainable and human-governed opportunity assessment.
Primary User
Transformation Analyst or Automation / AI Centre of Excellence Analyst.
Decision User
Transformation Lead or Centre of Excellence Lead.
Core Capabilities
Release 1 will support:
• business idea intake;
• Process Mining observation intake;
• interpretation of unstructured opportunity descriptions;
• extraction of business and process information;
• identification of missing information;
• generation of clarification questions;
• opportunity classification;
• indicative business-value assessment;
• indicative feasibility assessment;
• risk identification;
• evidence-strength indication;
• intervention recommendation;
• recommendation rationale;
• human review;
• human override with rationale; and
• final opportunity outcome.
Supported Intervention Recommendations
The product may recommend:
• no intervention;
• further assessment;
• process redesign;
• policy or procedural change;
• deterministic business rules;
• workflow automation;
• Robotic Process Automation;
• Predictive Machine Learning;
• Generative Artificial Intelligence;
• Agentic Artificial Intelligence; or
• a hybrid approach.
Release 1 Outcome
Release 1 should establish whether the core opportunity-qualification journey is sufficiently useful, understandable and trustworthy to justify further investment.
Evidence Required to Progress
Progression to Release 2 should require evidence that:
• users can submit and clarify opportunities successfully;
• the product identifies important information gaps;
• recommendations are relevant to the opportunity;
• reviewers understand the recommendation rationale;
• Artificial Intelligence does not consistently force-fit complex interventions;
• human overrides are visible and explainable; and
• the assessment provides more consistency or efficiency than the existing approach.

Release 2 - Opportunity Discovery and Prioritisation
Release Objective
Expand IdeaHub AI from qualification of one opportunity to discovery and comparison of multiple transformation opportunities.
Product Evolution
Release 2 will strengthen the role of Process Mining and introduce opportunity comparison.
Process Mining findings will provide evidence of:
• bottlenecks;
• rework loops;
• extended waiting time;
• repeated exceptions;
• manual hand-offs;
• process variation; and
• possible conformance issues.
IdeaHub AI will help convert these findings into candidate transformation opportunities.
Additional Capabilities
Release 2 may introduce:
• import of structured Process Mining findings;
• document and process-description analysis;
• consolidation of related process findings;
• comparison of multiple opportunities;
• configurable value, feasibility and risk criteria;
• opportunity ranking;
• candidate transformation backlog;
• draft business-case summaries;
• reviewer comments;
• assessment history;
• source references; and
• decision audit trail.
Prioritisation Approach
Opportunities may be compared using:
• expected business value;
• feasibility;
• risk;
• evidence strength;
• strategic alignment;
• expected effort; and
• dependency on other initiatives.
Artificial Intelligence may assist with interpretation and explanation.
Defined scoring, mandatory criteria and governance conditions should remain rules-based where practical.
Release 2 Outcome
Release 2 should demonstrate whether IdeaHub AI can support transparent comparison and prioritisation across a backlog of transformation opportunities.
Evidence Required to Progress
Progression to Release 3 should require evidence that:
• Process Mining findings can be converted into meaningful opportunities;
• comparable opportunities receive reasonably consistent assessments;
• users find the comparison useful for portfolio discussions;
• scoring logic remains explainable;
• reviewers can identify and correct inappropriate recommendations;
• ranking does not reward presentation quality over business evidence; and
• the product supports better prioritisation decisions than the existing approach.

Release 3 - Enterprise Transformation Intelligence
Release Objective
Evolve IdeaHub AI into an enterprise transformation decision-support product that connects opportunity discovery, qualification, prioritisation, governance and portfolio insight.
Additional Capabilities
Release 3 may introduce:
• direct integration with Process Mining platforms;
• integration with enterprise workflow and portfolio-management systems;
• organisation-specific assessment models;
• industry-specific assessment models;
• multi-level governance and approval workflows;
• portfolio dashboards;
• capacity and dependency visibility;
• opportunity lifecycle tracking;
• investment-theme analysis;
• benefit-realisation tracking;
• recommendation-outcome comparison;
• controlled learning from reviewer decisions;
• model and prompt monitoring;
• role-based access control; and
• enterprise audit and reporting.
Enterprise Intelligence
The product may provide insights such as:
• distribution of opportunity types;
• common process bottlenecks;
• concentration of transformation risk;
• recurring data-readiness gaps;
• business units generating the highest-value opportunities;
• differences between recommended and approved interventions;
• common reasons for rejection or deferral; and
• realised outcomes of previously selected opportunities.
These insights should support decision-making without removing human accountability.
Release 3 Outcome
Release 3 should establish IdeaHub AI as a governed enterprise capability for identifying where transformation investment is most likely to create value.

Roadmap Decision Principles
The following principles will guide roadmap decisions:
1. Validate Before Expanding
We will not expand into portfolio management before validating individual opportunity qualification.
2. Process Mining Is an Input, Not the Whole Product
We will initially use Process Mining findings as evidence for opportunity discovery.
Direct Process Mining integrations will be considered only when they create sufficient additional value.
3. Prefer Simpler Interventions
IdeaHub AI will not recommend a more complex Artificial Intelligence intervention where process redesign, rules or conventional automation can address the problem adequately.
4. Keep Human Accountability
Human reviewers will remain responsible for approving opportunities and transformation investment decisions.
5. Learn Without Uncontrolled Self-Modification
Reviewer feedback may inform future product improvement, but the product will not autonomously change its assessment criteria or governance rules.
6. Measure Decision Quality, Not Feature Count
Roadmap progression will depend on whether the product improves transformation decision-making, not on the number of capabilities added.

Current Roadmap Recommendation
Our current recommendation is to focus first on Release 1 and validate the core opportunity-qualification journey.
Release 2 and Release 3 represent possible product evolution and should proceed only if evidence demonstrates that users trust the assessment, understand the rationale and obtain value from more consistent transformation decisions.
