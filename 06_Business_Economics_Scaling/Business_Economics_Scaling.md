IdeaHub AI - Business, Economics & Scaling
Product: IdeaHub AI
Team: Team0022

1. Business Value
IdeaHub AI is intended to improve how enterprise transformation teams discover, qualify and prioritise transformation opportunities.
The product can create value by:
• reducing manual effort required to structure incomplete transformation ideas;
• identifying missing information earlier in the assessment;
• applying a more consistent assessment approach across opportunities;
• helping reviewers compare business value, feasibility, risk and evidence;
• reducing technology-first decision-making;
• converting Process Mining findings into assessable business opportunities;
• improving transparency of intervention recommendations; and
• creating a more traceable transformation decision process.
The expected value is not simply faster assessment.
The larger product objective is to improve how limited transformation funding and delivery capacity are directed towards opportunities that have stronger evidence of value and feasibility.

2. Customer and Buyer
The primary organisational customer for IdeaHub AI is an enterprise running a transformation, automation or Artificial Intelligence programme.
Potential organisational buyers include:
• Transformation Office;
• Automation Centre of Excellence;
• Artificial Intelligence Centre of Excellence;
• Digital Transformation function; and
• Enterprise Innovation function.
Primary users include Transformation Analysts, Automation or Artificial Intelligence Centre of Excellence analysts, Business Analysts and Solution Consultants.
Transformation Leads and governance teams remain responsible for final prioritisation and investment decisions.

3. Value Drivers
The main value drivers for IdeaHub AI are:
Assessment Efficiency
Reduce repeated manual effort involved in understanding, clarifying and structuring transformation ideas.
Assessment Consistency
Apply common assessment dimensions across opportunities rather than relying entirely on individual reviewer judgement.
Better Opportunity Quality
Identify important information gaps before an opportunity moves into detailed assessment or delivery planning.
Better Intervention Selection
Help teams determine whether a problem should be addressed through:
• process redesign;
• business rules;
• workflow automation;
• Robotic Process Automation;
• Predictive Machine Learning;
• Generative Artificial Intelligence;
• Agentic Artificial Intelligence; or
• another approach.
Process Mining Value Realisation
Help translate Process Mining observations into business opportunities that can be assessed for transformation investment.
Decision Traceability
Preserve the evidence, assumptions, Artificial Intelligence recommendation and human decision behind each opportunity assessment.

Product Operations

5. Build, Buy and API Economics
We do not propose building every component of IdeaHub AI internally.
Build
The organisation should own the differentiated product logic, including:
• opportunity-assessment framework;
• intervention taxonomy;
• deterministic scoring and controls;
• human-review workflow;
• explainability;
• governance logic; and
• decision traceability.
Buy / API
Foundation Artificial Intelligence capabilities can be consumed through an external Application Programming Interface rather than developing a foundation model.
This reduces initial model-development investment and allows the product team to focus on the enterprise transformation workflow.
Partner / Integrate
Existing Process Mining, workflow and enterprise platforms can be integrated where appropriate rather than replicating their capabilities.
The economic decision should therefore focus on owning the differentiated transformation decision capability while consuming commodity capabilities where practical.

6. Commercial Model
A future commercial version of IdeaHub AI could use an enterprise subscription model.
Possible commercial units may include:
• enterprise subscription;
• organisational or business-unit subscription;
• named-user licence;
• assessment-volume-based pricing; or
• a combination of fixed and usage-based pricing.
The appropriate pricing model should be validated against actual customer willingness to pay and the value created.
At this stage, we do not assign a specific product price because sufficient market evidence has not yet been established.

7. Unit Economics Considerations
The economics of an individual opportunity assessment should consider:
Cost to Serve =
Artificial Intelligence usage
• application infrastructure
• integration overhead
• human review
• governance and support allocation
An Artificial Intelligence-assisted assessment has economic value only if the additional cost is justified by improvement in:
• assessment effort;
• assessment quality;
• decision consistency;
• decision speed; or
• transformation investment quality.
A more advanced Artificial Intelligence model should therefore not automatically be preferred if a smaller model, deterministic rule or non-AI method can produce sufficient product value.

8. Scaling Considerations
Scaling IdeaHub AI involves more than increasing technical capacity.
The product must continue delivering its core promise as the number and variety of opportunities increase.
User Scale
The product may need to support:
• multiple Transformation Analysts;
• different business units;
• different transformation teams; and
• different governance structures.
Opportunity Scale
A larger volume of opportunities creates requirements for:
• comparison;
• prioritisation;
• search;
• auditability; and
• backlog management.
Industry Scale
Different industries may require different:
• assessment criteria;
• governance controls;
• data requirements;
• risk thresholds; and
• intervention patterns.
Artificial Intelligence Scale
Increased usage may increase:
• model usage costs;
• latency;
• monitoring requirements; and
• dependency on external model providers.
The product should use appropriately sized models and deterministic logic where sufficient.
Process Mining Scale
Different Process Mining platforms may produce findings in different formats.
IdeaHub AI should therefore focus on a common business representation of the process finding rather than becoming dependent on one Process Mining vendor.

9. Scaling Risks
Important scaling risks include:
Cost Growth
Artificial Intelligence usage cost may grow with the volume and complexity of assessments.
Model Dependency
Dependence on one external model provider may create availability, pricing or product-change risks.
Inconsistent Recommendations
As opportunity diversity increases, recommendation quality may vary across industries and process types.
Governance Complexity
Different organisational units may apply different assessment and approval policies.
Human Review Bottleneck
If every decision requires extensive manual review, human effort may become a scaling constraint.
Over-Customisation
Excessive customer- or industry-specific customisation may weaken the reusable core product.

10. Scaling Approach
IdeaHub AI should scale progressively.
First
Validate individual opportunity qualification.
Next
Support comparison and prioritisation across multiple opportunities and strengthen Process Mining-enabled discovery.
Later
Introduce:
• enterprise integrations;
• configurable assessment frameworks;
• industry-specific models;
• portfolio analytics;
• multi-level governance; and
• controlled learning from previous assessments.
Scaling decisions should follow evidence of product value rather than the availability of additional Artificial Intelligence capabilities.

11. Economic Principle
The core economic principle for IdeaHub AI is:
Use Artificial Intelligence only where its incremental value in interpretation, assessment or decision support exceeds its additional cost and governance burden.

The economic objective is therefore not to maximise Artificial Intelligence usage.
It is to improve transformation decision-making at a sustainable cost.
