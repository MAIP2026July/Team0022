IdeaHub AI - Initial TO-BE Workflow
Product: IdeaHub AI
Team: Team0022
Artefact: Initial TO-BE Workflow / Swimlane

1. Purpose
This workflow represents the initial TO-BE operating model for IdeaHub AI.
It shows how a transformation opportunity can originate either from:
1. a business-submitted idea; or
2. an improvement signal identified through Process Mining or process analysis.
IdeaHub AI then supports interpretation, clarification, assessment and intervention recommendation before an authorised human makes the final decision.
This is an initial product workflow and may be refined as the product, requirements and user experience evolve.

2. Opportunity Entry Paths
Path A - Business-Led Opportunity
A Business Stakeholder or Process Owner identifies:
• a business problem;
• process inefficiency;
• automation opportunity;
• Artificial Intelligence opportunity; or
• other transformation need.
The stakeholder submits the available business and process information to IdeaHub AI.
Path B - Process-Led Opportunity
Process Mining or process analysis identifies an operational signal such as:
• a bottleneck;
• rework loop;
• extended waiting time;
• repeated exception;
• manual hand-off; or
• process deviation.
The Process Analyst provides the finding and available supporting evidence.
IdeaHub AI treats the finding as an opportunity signal that still requires business qualification.

3. IdeaHub AI Interpretation
IdeaHub AI interprets the submitted information and structures the opportunity.
The product identifies:
• business process;
• stakeholder;
• business problem;
• pain point;
• frequency or volume where available;
• expected outcome;
• existing evidence; and
• relevant constraints.
Artificial Intelligence interpretation is presented to the Transformation Analyst for review.

4. Clarification and Evidence
IdeaHub AI identifies important information gaps.
Examples may include:
• process volume;
• cycle time;
• manual effort;
• exception rate;
• affected users;
• data availability;
• system dependencies; or
• business impact.
IdeaHub AI generates context-specific clarification questions.
The Transformation Analyst can:
• answer using available information;
• request information from the Business Stakeholder or Process Owner;
• record the information as unknown; or
• continue with the limitation explicitly recorded.
IdeaHub AI should not fabricate missing business information.

5. Opportunity Assessment
Once sufficient information is available, IdeaHub AI supports an indicative assessment across:
Business Value
Potential contribution to business, customer or employee outcomes.
Feasibility
Practicality based on process, data, technology and integration considerations.
Risk
Material business, privacy, security, regulatory, operational and adoption risks.
Evidence Strength
Quality and completeness of information supporting the assessment.
The Transformation Analyst can review and challenge the assessment.

6. Intervention Recommendation
IdeaHub AI recommends an appropriate intervention.
The recommendation may include:
• no intervention;
• further assessment;
• process redesign;
• policy or procedural change;
• deterministic business rules;
• workflow automation;
• Robotic Process Automation;
• Predictive Machine Learning;
• Generative Artificial Intelligence;
• Agentic Artificial Intelligence; or
• a hybrid approach.
IdeaHub AI should prefer the least complex intervention that adequately addresses the problem.
The recommendation includes:
• supporting evidence;
• rationale;
• assumptions;
• information gaps; and
• material risks.

7. Human Review
The Transformation Analyst reviews the Artificial Intelligence-assisted assessment.
The analyst can:
• correct the interpretation;
• modify the assessment;
• add evidence;
• challenge assumptions; and
• recommend a different intervention.
The reviewed opportunity is then submitted to the Transformation Lead or governance authority.

8. Governance Decision
The Transformation Lead or governance authority reviews:
• opportunity definition;
• supporting evidence;
• value;
• feasibility;
• risk;
• evidence confidence;
• IdeaHub AI recommendation;
• analyst modifications; and
• remaining assumptions.
The authorised reviewer can:
• Accept;
• Modify;
• Request More Information; or
• Reject.
The final opportunity outcome may be:
• Reject;
• Refine;
• Explore;
• Prototype;
• Pilot; or
• Candidate for Prioritisation.
IdeaHub AI supports the decision.
Human governance remains responsible for the final outcome.

9. Feedback and Traceability
The workflow records:
• original opportunity information;
• Artificial Intelligence interpretation;
• clarification responses;
• assessment;
• recommendation;
• human modifications;
• final decision; and
• decision rationale.
Reviewer overrides and corrections can provide evidence for future improvement of IdeaHub AI.
The product should not autonomously modify its assessment or governance rules based on reviewer activity without appropriate validation.

10. Workflow Principle
The central workflow principle is:
Artificial Intelligence structures, clarifies, assesses and recommends. Humans validate, challenge and decide.
