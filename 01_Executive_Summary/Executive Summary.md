IdeaHub AI - Executive Summary
Team: Team0022
Course: MBA ZG583 - Management of AI Products
Team Members: Ravi Theja Kumar Pothulluru and Prathamesh Sandeep Nigade
Problem
Enterprise transformation teams receive ideas for process improvement, workflow automation, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Generative AI and Agentic AI through workshops, emails, spreadsheets, process reviews and discussions with business teams.
Existing processes can also contain improvement opportunities that have not yet been formally identified. Process Mining and process analysis can expose bottlenecks, rework, delays, exceptions and repetitive activities that may become candidates for transformation.
The challenge is that these ideas and process findings still need to be qualified before organisations commit limited transformation funding and delivery capacity.
Initial assessments can be time-consuming and inconsistent because:
• idea descriptions may be incomplete or unstructured;
• different reviewers may apply different assessment criteria;
• Process Mining findings do not automatically translate into business opportunities;
• business value, feasibility, risk and data readiness may not be evaluated consistently;
• technology choices may be proposed before the underlying problem is understood; and
• the rationale behind prioritisation decisions may not be easily traceable.
The opportunity is therefore not simply to generate more transformation ideas, but to improve how organisations discover, qualify and prioritise them.
Target Users
The primary users of IdeaHub AI are:
• Transformation Analysts;
• Automation or AI Centre of Excellence analysts;
• Business Analysts; and
• Solution Consultants involved in opportunity assessment.
Transformation Leads and governance reviewers act as key decision-makers by reviewing assessments and deciding whether an opportunity should progress.
Business process owners and idea submitters provide the business context and evidence required for assessment.
Product Concept
IdeaHub AI is an AI-assisted transformation discovery, qualification and prioritisation copilot.
The product supports two entry paths:
1. Idea-led assessment
A business stakeholder submits an improvement, automation or AI opportunity.
IdeaHub AI interprets the submission, identifies missing information and helps structure the opportunity for assessment.
2. Process-led discovery
A Process Mining finding or process observation identifies a bottleneck, rework loop, delay, exception or other improvement signal.
IdeaHub AI helps convert the finding into a candidate transformation opportunity before it is assessed.
For either path, IdeaHub AI supports assessment across:
• business value;
• feasibility;
• data readiness;
• implementation complexity;
• risk; and
• available evidence.
The product can recommend an appropriate intervention such as:
• process redesign;
• policy or procedural change;
• deterministic business rules;
• workflow automation;
• Robotic Process Automation;
• Predictive Machine Learning;
• Generative AI;
• Agentic AI;
• a hybrid approach; or
• further assessment where evidence is insufficient.
IdeaHub AI provides a recommendation and rationale, while the final prioritisation and investment decision remains with the human reviewer.
Why AI?
A form, spreadsheet or deterministic scoring model can capture structured information and calculate predefined scores, but transformation ideas are often expressed as unstructured business narratives.
AI can add value by:
• interpreting differently written opportunity descriptions;
• extracting relevant business and process information;
• identifying missing information;
• generating clarification questions;
• classifying opportunity types;
• analysing Process Mining observations expressed in business terms;
• comparing possible intervention approaches; and
• producing a concise rationale for the recommended approach.
AI will not be used for every product decision.
Deterministic rules will be preferred for defined scoring logic, mandatory controls, thresholds and governance conditions.
Human review will remain mandatory before an opportunity is committed to a transformation backlog or receives an investment decision.
Minimum Viable Product
The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) will demonstrate one narrow decision loop.
A user will:
1. submit a transformation opportunity or Process Mining observation;
2. provide basic process and business context;
3. receive clarification questions where information is incomplete;
4. receive an indicative value, feasibility and risk assessment;
5. receive a recommended intervention with supporting rationale; and
6. review, accept, modify or reject the recommendation.
The prototype will use synthetic demonstration scenarios and mocked AI responses where appropriate.
It will demonstrate the intended user experience, decision logic, explainability and Human-in-the-Loop control. It will not claim to demonstrate production-scale AI accuracy, enterprise integration or validated financial benefits.
